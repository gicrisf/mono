Documentation pages carry a lot of structure: navigation, a title, sections, code, tables, and cross-references. A plain column hides that structure. This theme makes it visible.

## The Sheet

Each page sits on a paper sheet on a neutral desk. The border and hard shadow separate the reading surface from the browser chrome without adding decoration inside the text.

## The Rules

Dotted rules separate groups. Solid rules mark controls and tables. Dashed rules divide article sections. The three weights are enough to distinguish structure without a second color.

## The Labels

Monospace labels name code, navigation, metadata, and buttons. The label is small, uppercase, and spaced, so it reads as a printed annotation.

## The Result

The page looks like a catalog page rather than an application. That suits reference material, which is read in short passes and revisited.
